Article
with sticky aside
blocks/Article.tsx

Long-form reading at 72 characters with a sticky aside.

ARTICLE

Long-form reading layout

Lead paragraph, one size up from body. It summarises the article in two sentences.

Body text at 17px on a 72-character measure. Line length is what makes long text readable; width alone does not. This column never grows past 72ch even on a wide screen.

Subheading inside the article

Another paragraph. Lists inside prose keep their bullets, unlike lists elsewhere on the site.
